Who Am I?

Peter is a passionate web developer and renewable energy expert based in Ibadan, Nigeria. With six years of hands-on experience as a Solar Installation Expert, he has built a reputation for delivering reliable and cost-effective energy solutions to both residential and commercial clients. He is currently expanding his expertise by mastering full-stack web development, studying HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js and more, with the goal of building digital solutions that address the real needs of his community.

My Journey

Beyond coding, Peter is driven by a deep desire to impact the next generation. He is dedicated to identifying and mentoring young people between the ages of 16 and 25 in Ibadan, equipping them with web development skills to build apps and websites that solve everyday problems in their society. He believes that technology, like solar energy, should be accessible to everyone.
